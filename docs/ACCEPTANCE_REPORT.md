## Performance and balance limits

The 30,000-round fixture uses four crawlers plus 50 enemies with debug survival stats. Median resolution was 18.92ms, p95 43.76ms and maximum **16,227ms** in a shared virtualized cloud host. There were no deferred events or failed resource invariants. The maximum is a material long-tail concern; a standard desktop baseline has not been established. Fifty replays of a previously captured slow round had one checksum and max 57.12ms; this does not explain every observed stall.

Assisted campaigns prove route progression, not difficulty. The small ordinary solo sample won one alternate-route seed and lost three combat-route seeds on floors 2–3. An additional ordinary four-crawler sample won seeds 0 (alternate) and 3 (combat), lost seed 2 on floor 6 and exposed a bot exit-congestion policy on seed 1. Legal step-aside diagnostics identified bot gate congestion; after a routing-policy fix seed 1 reached final VICTORY without assistance. The initial failed sample is preserved. These cases are neither a reliable win rate nor human balance evidence. The 75–120 minute run-length target and friends-playtest scores are unmeasured.

The first browser endurance attempt stalled after about 23 minutes; its partial evidence is retained as browser-soak-initial.json. That run did not pass. Connection recovery and transport retry tests were added, and the repeat passed 1,801.48 seconds with 3,384 actions, zero browser errors, matching final revisions and 7.15–7.71MiB final heaps. That long run began before final targeting/camera/watchdog polish; the final client also passed a separate 62.76-second four-context smoke and all six browser scenarios. Do not infer the first stall's cause from those fixes: the original fixed-cycle driver lacked enough diagnostics to identify it.

All gold scenarios are represented in tests/unit/{engine,progression,reactions}.test.ts and tests/integration/{network,persistence}.test.ts. See TEST_REPORT.txt and CURRENT_STATE.md for the final command outcomes, and docs/ISSUES.md for open acceptance work.
